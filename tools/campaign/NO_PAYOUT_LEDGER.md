# No-Payout Ledger

Confirmed, exploitation-ready findings where no active bug bounty program
covers the target. Vulnerabilities here are real and fully documented but have
no monetary submission path at time of entry.

Submission should be deferred until either:
(a) the project is financially self-sustaining and responsible disclosure
    is worth the time cost, or
(b) a bounty program is launched by the vendor or a downstream deployer.

Do NOT spend time on these while paid targets remain unworked.
